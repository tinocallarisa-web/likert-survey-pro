watermark (small multiples: first 4 panels). Intentional, not missing gating. In reading
view and where licences cannot be read, the FREE result is drawn, no watermark.

PRIVACY/NETWORK
No network calls, telemetry, cookies or storage; privileges: []. Start screen links open
only on click via host.launchUrl. No innerHTML: createElementNS and textContent.
ESLint clean, npm audit 0.

LICENSING
IVisualLicenseManager only; no own licensing UI or key field. Requested once after the
first render, never blocking it; failure stays Free, no prompts. Active or Warning.
clearLicenseNotification, notifyFeatureBlocked, then notifyLicenseRequired after ~10 s.

RENDERING EVENTS
Each update() emits one renderingStarted and one Finished/Failed. Licence repaint, setup
guide and fetchMoreData requests do not call update(). allowInteractions read from
hostCapabilities.
